OUR PROCESS

Advanced Water Purification Process

Every drop of Purifite Paani passes through multiple purification stages before reaching our customers, ensuring purity, safety, and refreshing taste.

01

Raw Water

Carefully sourced water enters our purification plant.

02

Sand & Carbon Filter

Removes suspended particles, odor and unwanted impurities.

03

RO Purification

Advanced Reverse Osmosis removes dissolved impurities.

04

UV Sterilization

Ultraviolet treatment eliminates bacteria and viruses.

05

Ozonization

Ozone treatment maintains freshness and hygiene.

06

Automatic Bottling

Hygienic filling, sealing, inspection and packing.
