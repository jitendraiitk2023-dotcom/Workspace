OUR PROCESS

Advanced Water Purification

Purifite Paani follows a multi-stage purification process to ensure safe and healthy drinking water.

Sand Filtration

Removes dust, mud and suspended particles.

Activated Carbon

Removes odor, chlorine and organic impurities.

RO Purification

Removes dissolved salts and harmful contaminants.

UV Sterilization

Eliminates bacteria, viruses and microorganisms.

Ozonization

Keeps water fresh and hygienic inside every bottle.

Automatic Bottling

Completely hygienic filling and sealed packaging.

QUALITY ASSURANCE
